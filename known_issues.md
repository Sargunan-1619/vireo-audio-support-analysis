# Known Issues / Limitations

- 139 tickets were created before the stated Jan 2025 analysis window.
- 2,379 rows have resolution before creation; 2,575 have resolution before first response. These are concentrated in legacy data.
- Legacy transfer values are blank by design and are not imputed as zero.
- Existing tags are weak labels.
- Validation is a 110-ticket stratified analyst review, not a production-scale benchmark.
- No FTE recommendation is made because occupancy, actual handle time, staffing hours, backlog and shrinkage are unavailable.
- The categorizer intentionally favors precision and reproducibility over broad fuzzy matching.
